── Common rgba() Transparency Patterns ────────────────────

  Black overlays:
    0.04 – 0.08   Very subtle (hover tints)
    0.1 – 0.15    Subtle (borders, shadows)
    0.2 – 0.3     Medium (overlays, modals)
    0.4 – 0.6     Heavy (dark overlays, backdrops)

  Gold overlays:
    0.03 – 0.05   Ultra subtle (backgrounds)
    0.08 – 0.15   Subtle (hover, focus rings)
    0.2 – 0.3     Medium (borders, badges)
    0.8            Heavy (buttons, solid gold)

  White overlays (on dark bg):
    0.08 – 0.15   Subtle (dividers on dark)
    0.2 – 0.3     Medium (text on dark)
    0.4 – 0.6     Strong (primary text on dark)
    0.85 – 0.95   Near-opaque (cards on dark)

  Green overlays:
    rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1) – 0.2   Success tints

  Red overlays:
    rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.1) – 0.3    Error tints

  Blue overlays:
    rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.06) – 0.12  Info tints
